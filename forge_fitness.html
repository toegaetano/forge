Step 2 · Training

Train for the goal, not the template.

Your weekly structure is built from your schedule, priority muscles, equipment, and flagged constraints. Treat it as a starting draft—not medical clearance.

Step 3 · Nutrition

Fuel the outcome.

Targets are estimates. Real-world weight trends, training performance, hunger, recovery, and clinician guidance take priority over false precision.

Step 4 · Feedback loop

Measure trends, not noise.

Record weigh-ins and review changes at a sensible interval. The system suggests small adjustments; it won't silently rewrite your plan.

Data controls

What this prototype does not do yet

  • It does not diagnose injuries or automatically approve painful exercises.
  • It does not sync across devices or support coach/client accounts.
  • It does not replace a clinician, registered dietitian, or individualized professional review.
  • It uses rule-based personalization, not a validated clinical or performance prediction model.
FORGE is a prototype for education and planning. It is not medical care. Pain, dizziness, chest symptoms, or worsening daily function warrant appropriate professional evaluation.
